## How do I generate my Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from CAMS?

To get your CAS, follow these steps:
Visit the official
CAMS website.
Click on 'MF Investor.'
Select 'Statement' and then choose 'CAS - CAMS+KFintech.'
Enter the following details:
Statement Type: Detailed
Period: Specific Period (From 1st Jan 1990 to the present date)
Folio Listing: With zero balance folios
Email ID: Linked to your investments
Password: PAN number in uppercase
Submit the details.
You'll receive your CAS via email from
donotreply@camsonline.com
in PDF format.
You can click on the 'CAMS' button below to go directly to the CAS - CAMS+KFintech page.
